How JessEDU works

Find your page

A short placement quiz puts you on the chapter that fits, so nothing is dull and nothing is impossible.

Work through it

Short lessons followed by games. Word matching, listening, scrambles, and timed rounds.

Keep the streak

Return each day, collect experience, and unlock the next chapter of the book.
